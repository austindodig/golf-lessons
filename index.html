How it teaches

Cause and effect, not tips.

Golf instruction is full of feels that work for one player and wreck the next. Every lesson here starts from what the ball actually does at impact, then builds the motion that produces it. The tools let you move the numbers and watch the result.

Ball Flight Lab

Set club speed, attack angle, path and face. Watch drag and Magnus lift shape the flight, read carry, apex, spin and curve, and learn the nine ball flights by producing them.

Swing Plane Viewer

An orbitable swing driven by a double-pendulum model on Hogan's pane of glass. Scrub from address to finish through P1 to P10, with shoulder turn, hip turn and wrist set read out live.

Green Reader

A green that tilts, a ball that rolls under gravity and friction, and a hole that only accepts putts arriving at the right pace. Read the break, pick a line, and watch it happen.

The curriculum

One lesson for every swing.

Work through in order or jump straight to the shot that costs you strokes. Your progress and quiz scores stay in this browser.

The Setup

The Full Swing

The full swing as a single motion, read through the P1 to P10 checkpoints: plane, sequence, width, tempo, impact, and what your finish tells you.

The Driver

Why the driver is the only club you strike on the upswing, how to set up for it, and how launch, spin and strike location turn clubhead speed into carry.

Full swing25 min
04

Irons

The descending strike that makes irons work: low point ahead of the ball, shaft lean and weight forward at impact, plus honest distance gapping and trajectory control.

Full swing25 min
05

Wedges & Pitching

Build a body-driven pitching motion, then use the clock system to turn four wedges into twelve reliable carry numbers you can trust from 30 to 100 yards.

Short game30 min
06

Chipping

Learn the chip as a lofted putting stroke, pick a landing spot every time, and use carry-to-roll ratios to choose the club that gets the ball rolling soonest.

Short game25 min
07

Bunker Play

Understand bounce, open the face before you grip, splash a dollar bill of sand from two inches behind the ball, and finish every swing.

Short game25 min
08

Putting

Setup, the pendulum stroke, launch and roll, pace and the lag zone, reading slope to an aim point, and three drills that make it repeatable.

On the green30 min
09

The Ball Flight Laws

The modern ball flight laws: the face sets the start, face-to-path sets the curve, gear effect, spin loft, why a slice happens, and how to diagnose yourself from divot and flight.

Course management

Play the hole backwards.

Scroll to fly the hole. Start from the pin, find the side of the green that leaves an uphill putt, pick the fairway spot that opens that angle, then choose the club off the tee that reaches it.

1 · PinStart here. The green tilts back to front, so the putt you want is uphill from below the hole.
2 · GreenTwo bunkers guard the right. Missing left or long leaves a chip up the slope; missing right leaves sand.
3 · ApproachFrom the left half of the fairway the green opens up and the bunkers sit out of the picture.
4 · TeeThe fairway is widest at 230 yards, short of the pond. A 3-wood to that spot beats a driver into trouble.
